How to convert SVG to PNG

  1. Drop one or more SVG files into the box above, or click to choose them.
  2. Choose an output size — 1×, 2× or 4× the SVG's own size, or an exact width in pixels.
  3. Keep the background transparent or switch to white.
  4. Download each PNG individually, or everything at once as a ZIP file.

Why convert SVG to PNG?

SVG is perfect for editing and scaling, but some platforms — social networks, marketplaces, email newsletters and many office apps — only accept raster images. Exporting a high-resolution PNG lets you use your vector artwork anywhere while keeping edges sharp at the size you need.

Is it private?

Yes. Files are rendered by your own browser and never uploaded to a server. When you close the page, they are gone.

Which size should I choose?

An SVG has no fixed resolution, so you decide how many pixels the PNG gets. 1× uses the width and height written in the SVG file and suits on-screen use at normal size. 2× doubles every dimension and is a good default for sharp results on high-density (Retina) screens. 4× is best for print, large banners or when you plan to crop the image later. Choose Width when a platform asks for an exact size, such as 1080 px for a social post; the height is calculated automatically so the image keeps its proportions.

Transparent or white background?

Keep the background transparent for logos, icons and stickers that will sit on top of other colours. Choose white when the destination does not support transparency — some marketplaces, email clients and document editors show transparent areas as black.

Troubleshooting

  • A file shows “Could not read this SVG”. The file may be damaged or rely on external images or fonts the browser cannot load. Open it in a design tool, convert text to outlines, embed any images and save it again.
  • Text looks different. If the SVG uses a font that is not installed on your device, the browser substitutes another one. Converting text to paths before exporting fixes this.
  • The ZIP does not download. Some browsers block automatic downloads; allow downloads for this site, or save each PNG individually.
