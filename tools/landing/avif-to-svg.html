What is an AVIF file?

AVIF is a modern compressed image format that is often smaller than JPG or WEBP at similar quality. It is increasingly used on websites, so images you save from the web may arrive as .avif files. Like PNG and JPG, AVIF is a raster format: it stores pixels and blurs when enlarged. Converting it to SVG gives you scalable vector artwork.

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari can decode AVIF, so the converter works in all of them. If your browser is older and the file will not load, open the AVIF in an image viewer, save it as PNG, and convert the PNG instead.

Should I convert AVIF to SVG or keep it?

Keep AVIF for photographs on a website; convert to SVG for logos, icons, illustrations and anything you need to print, cut or scale. Photos can be vectorized with the Photograph type, but the result is a stylised poster effect rather than a realistic copy.
