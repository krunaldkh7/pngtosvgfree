Does converting PNG to SVG keep transparency?

Yes — with PNG to SVG Free, transparent areas of a PNG stay transparent in the SVG because only the visible artwork is traced into paths. Some converters fill transparent areas with white or black, which ruins logos, stickers and cut files. Here, fully transparent pixels are excluded from tracing, so the SVG contains only your design.

How do I convert a transparent PNG to SVG?

  1. Upload the transparent PNG

    Drop the PNG into the converter. Transparency is detected automatically.

  2. Check the edges

    Use the split view and zoom to check soft edges. Raise speckle removal if faint semi-transparent pixels create stray shapes.

  3. Remove any extra colours

    If part of the image should be transparent but is not, click the × on that colour in the palette.

  4. Download

    Save the SVG. It will display with a clear background in browsers, design tools and cutting software.

How do I remove a white background from a PNG or JPG?

Convert the image, then click the × on the white (or other background) colour in the palette — all of its paths are deleted, leaving a transparent background. This works for PNGs that were saved without transparency and for JPGs, which can never be transparent. It is often easier and more accurate than erasing the background in a photo editor first.

What about soft shadows and semi-transparent edges?

Vectors use solid colours, so soft shadows and semi-transparent glows are either converted into solid shapes or removed. Anti-aliased edges — the slightly see-through pixels that make edges look smooth — are handled automatically by the converter's edge clean-up. Large soft shadows are best removed before tracing, or deleted afterwards by marking their colour transparent.

How can I check the SVG is really transparent?

Open the SVG in a browser tab with a dark theme, or place it on a coloured shape in a design tool — any unwanted background will show immediately. In the converter, the split view also shows the SVG on a neutral checkerboard-style stage so you can spot leftover background shapes before downloading.

Will my transparent PNG get a white background in the SVG?

No, transparent areas stay transparent. Only visible artwork is traced into paths.

Can I make a JPG transparent with this tool?

Yes, convert the JPG and click × on the background colour. The SVG will have a transparent background even though JPG cannot store transparency.

Why is there a faint outline around my design?

Semi-transparent edge pixels were traced as a separate colour. Lower the colour count or raise speckle removal, or remove that colour from the palette.

Does SVG support transparency?

Yes, any area of an SVG without a shape is transparent, and shapes can also have partial opacity.
